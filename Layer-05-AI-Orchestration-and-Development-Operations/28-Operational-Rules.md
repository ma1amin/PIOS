# Operational Rules

1. Every externally sourced fact retains provenance.
2. Missing factual data remains unknown rather than fabricated.
3. Organic ranking cannot be silently influenced by commercial payment.
4. Sponsored results are clearly labeled.
5. Trust calculations are versioned.
6. Ranking configurations are versioned.
7. Security controls remain active in all execution modes.
8. Privacy controls remain active in all execution modes.
9. External source failure degrades evidence coverage rather than corrupting canonical truth.
10. Material mutations generate audit events.
11. Generic agents do not receive direct unrestricted database access.
12. Production credentials are not exposed to AI prompts.
13. Destructive operations require explicit authorization and recovery planning.
14. Business claiming does not grant control over independent ratings, reviews, trust, or community history.
15. Low-confidence entity matches are not automatically merged.
16. Stale data is not presented as current without a freshness indicator.
17. AI output is validated before it becomes structured platform data.
18. Security incidents take precedence over feature delivery.
19. User privacy choices are preserved through downstream analytics.
20. High-impact uncertainty is escalated to a human decision owner.
