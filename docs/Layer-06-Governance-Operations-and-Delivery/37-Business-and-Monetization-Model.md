# Business and Monetization Model

## Consumer
Core place discovery should remain broadly accessible. Potential premium features may include advanced saved intelligence, specialized comparison tools, alerts, or professional-grade research where consumer value is demonstrated.

## Business
Possible paid capabilities:
- Verified/claimed profile administration.
- Branch analytics.
- reputation intelligence.
- customer feedback insights.
- data-quality alerts.
- competitive category analytics using permitted aggregated data.
- response/workflow tools.
- API integrations.

Payment does not purchase trust or organic rank.

## API and Developer
Potential tiers:
- Place lookup.
- Search.
- category intelligence.
- trust metadata.
- enterprise batch access.
- analytics feeds.

Pricing can combine subscription, request volume, and enterprise contracts.

## Enterprise
Use cases:
- Branch network intelligence.
- market coverage.
- service-network selection.
- vendor/location assessment.
- geographic benchmarking.
- regional expansion analysis.

## Commercial Integrity
Maintain a formal policy:
- Sponsored placement is labeled.
- Business subscription does not increase trust.
- Organic ranking models exclude payment.
- Commercial teams cannot directly override trust/ranking.
- Exceptions require governance and audit.

## Unit Economics
Track:
- Data acquisition cost.
- Search infrastructure cost.
- AI inference cost.
- map/geocoding cost.
- moderation cost.
- customer acquisition cost.
- business support cost.
- revenue per paid account/API customer.

AI usage should be optimized through deterministic logic, caching, smaller models, batching, and model routing where quality permits.
