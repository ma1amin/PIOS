# Competitive Positioning

## Reference Categories
PIOS can learn from established review/reputation platforms such as Trustpilot, REVIEWS.io, Trustmary, and Lipscore, while addressing a different primary decision problem.

## PIOS Position
PIOS is not designed as a generic review widget platform. Its core problem is physical-place decision intelligence.

## Differentiation Dimensions

### Geographic Context
A place recommendation includes distance, area, and geographic suitability.

### Canonical Place Identity
Multiple source representations are reconciled into a governed PIOS identity.

### Multi-Source Evidence
Evidence remains source-specific and attributable instead of collapsing everything into an unexplained number.

### Trust Intelligence
Trust assesses confidence in evidence and data quality independently from customer satisfaction.

### Contextual Ranking
The best place changes with user requirements. Ranking therefore considers intent, attributes, geography, evidence, and trust.

### Community Intelligence
Community contributions focus on structured local facts and useful experience signals, not engagement volume alone.

### Explanation
Users receive reasons and limitations, not only a sorted list.

### Arabic/MENA
Arabic, RTL, mixed-language search, regional taxonomy, and local geography are foundational.

## Strategic Rule
PIOS should compete on decision quality, evidence transparency, and local intelligence. It should not chase feature parity with every review platform when a feature does not improve the place-decision problem.
