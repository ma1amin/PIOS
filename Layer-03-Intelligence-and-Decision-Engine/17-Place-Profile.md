# Place Profile

## Canonical Structure
A place profile is the primary evidence page for a physical place.

## Sections
### Identity
- Canonical name.
- Alternate names.
- Category/subcategory.
- Organization/brand relationship.

### Location
- Address.
- Map position.
- District/city.
- Distance from user when permission exists.
- Location confidence.

### Services and Attributes
Schema-driven attributes appropriate to the category.

### Hours
- Published hours.
- Source.
- Last verified time.
- Conflict state if sources disagree.

### Contact
- Phone.
- Website.
- Supported contact methods.
- Provenance.

### Ratings
Source-specific ratings and counts where display rights permit. PIOS must not imply that ratings from different methodologies are directly equivalent without normalization context.

### Review Intelligence
Derived themes, trends, and aspect summaries with evidence constraints.

### Trust
Current trust state, evidence coverage, and key reasons.

### Community
Validated corrections, useful structured signals, Q&A, and reports as permitted.

### Freshness
Show whether important information is current, aging, stale, or uncertain.

## Business Claiming
Claiming proves an account’s authorized relationship with the place. It does not prove that every business-provided statement is independently verified.

Business edits should be tagged internally as business-provided and pass validation appropriate to the field.

## History
Material changes such as address, closure, merge, ownership/brand relation, or major verified corrections should maintain history for audit and data-quality purposes.
